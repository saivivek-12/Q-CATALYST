How Quantum-Assisted Screening Works

Architecture of the VQE simulation and screening pipeline

01

Active-Site Definition

Parameterises d-band orbital offset, coordination number, and local charge into effective model parameters.

02

Hamiltonian & VQE

Builds 2-qubit SparsePauliOp and solves ground state using Qiskit RealAmplitudes ansatz + SciPy BFGS.

03

Exact Validation

Diagonalises identical Hamiltonian matrix using exact eigensolver to guarantee error < 10⁻⁵ eV.

04

HER Proxy & Decision

Calculates ΔG_H* = ΔE_H* + 0.24 eV and categorises into SHORTLIST, BORDERLINE, or NOT SHORTLISTED.
